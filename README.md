# HR Executive Overview & Attrition Analytics

An interactive spreadsheet dashboard analyzing workforce demographics, monthly compensation patterns, employee satisfaction, and attrition risk across an organization of **1,473 employees** (**237 total departures**, **16.1% attrition rate**).

## Key Metrics at a Glance
- **Total Employees:** 1,473
- **Total Departures:** 237
- **Attrition Rate:** 16.1%
- **Average Monthly Income:** $6,500
- **Average Age:** 36.9 years
- **Average Job Satisfaction:** 2.73

## Dashboard Views & Interactive Controls
- **Dynamic Filters:** Department, Job Role, Age Group, Gender, Business Travel, and Job Satisfaction.
- **Workforce & Turnover Breakdown:** Departures by operating department, headcount by age group, and gender distribution.
- **Compensation vs. Age Progression:** Comparison of monthly income across employee age profiles.
- **Attrition Risk Analysis:** Turnover segmented by job satisfaction levels and business travel frequency, paired with a role-level risk heatmap.

## Design & Visual Approach
- Built with clean layout hierarchy, consistent visual styling, removed chart gridlines and redundant legends, and connected multi-select filters so stakeholders can explore workforce and retention drivers without visual clutter.
- Created manually without AI automation or unverified external claims.
